# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People deciding whether to download Hikki for their Mac, iPhone or iPad — the same individual note-keepers and
language learners the app serves (see the root `PRODUCT.md`). Inferred from the site's copy and structure; not yet
confirmed with the user.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site at hikki.yancey.app: shows the app, says what it is for, and links to the App Store once the
listing exists (`APP_STORE_URL` in `src/site.ts`; "Coming to the App Store" meanwhile).

## Positioning

The root `PRODUCT.md`'s two claims, in the visitor's words: your notes stay files you own; AI with your own keys.

## Operating Context

TanStack Start + Solid, SSR on Vercel; its own Mondrian palette (not the app's tokens); light / dark / system theme;
screenshots in `public/shots/` as WebP with dark twins, made by `bun run shots`.

The screenshots above, made from the real app. No testimonials, press, customers or metrics — none may be invented.
